# Trash Duplicate Resolution Offers Restore, Continue, or Dismiss

Trash Duplicate should present explicit resolution actions rather than
automatically restoring deleted assets or leaving users with a passive warning.
The available actions should be Restore Instead, Continue as New, and Dismiss
Trash Duplicate. Continue as New remains available from Promotion Sheet and
promotes the current candidate as a new Design Asset; Restore Instead restores
the matched Asset Trash item and moves the current candidate out of active
review through the recoverable candidate cleanup path; Dismiss Trash Duplicate
resolves only the weak duplicate signal without restoring, deleting, or merging
anything.
