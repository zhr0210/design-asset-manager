# Asset Ownership Conversion Keeps One Asset And Complete Original

Healthy and recovering Design Assets may explicitly use **Convert to Managed Asset** or **Convert to Referenced Asset** without reimporting, duplicating or changing their user-authored organization. Conversion binds the current Design Asset, Asset File Ownership, Source Content Generation and complete Original Asset; a Compound Original always converts every primary, required and included optional member together with the same Compound Original Identity and manifest. Exact byte/member equivalence preserves Source Content Generation and its current preview/analysis evidence, while any byte, format or manifest difference is not ownership conversion and must first complete its normal replacement, Attach Companion or Remove Optional Companion decision.

Convert to Managed Asset copies every current user-owned source member into operation-owned staging under the Managed Originals Directory, applies the normal managed identities, names, path, capacity and Storage Safety Reserve rules, and verifies the complete single-file or compound result before one atomic ownership, inventory and current-path commit. The external source members remain untouched and user-owned by default. After commit they become **Retained Ownership Conversion Sources**: provenance-visible but no longer used by preview, analysis, Original Handoff or source operations, and excluded from automatic discovery/readmission in the same library until the user explicitly releases that exclusion. They never enter Full Library Backup or library cleanup merely because the managed copy is current.

Convert to Referenced Asset first publishes the exact complete managed original to a user-selected safe external destination and verifies every byte, real format, declared member role and relationship there. Publication normally copies through verified destination staging; ADR 0356 may instead explicitly adopt an already present complete identical destination without publishing duplicate bytes. It refuses overwrite, silent unique naming, primary-only output and incompatible layout. Only then may one atomic commit replace managed ownership/inventory with the complete referenced relationship and Source Tree projection. The former library-owned file or Compound Original Directory becomes **Managed Ownership Conversion Residue** rather than being deleted during the ownership commit; it is not a current source, preview/analysis input, handoff payload, backup generation or second asset, cannot be removed by cache/storage pressure, and remains explicitly recoverable or cleanable after the new referenced source revalidates.

Before the ownership commit, cancellation leaves the original ownership authoritative and may remove only proven operation-owned staging. Once commit begins it is reconciled rather than force-stopped; a proven commit keeps the new ownership authoritative even when old managed cleanup or retained-external-source resolution remains pending. Unproven commit, target publication, inventory or relationship evidence enters **Asset Ownership Conversion Recovery** and never creates simultaneous current managed and referenced ownership, repeats an ambiguous destructive request or guesses authority from file location. Full Library Backup follows only the proven current ownership: managed bytes are included after Convert to Managed Asset, while Convert to Referenced Asset stores relationships but not source bytes.

ADR 0284 remains the recovery specialization: a missing managed original may convert only to an explicitly selected complete byte-equivalent referenced source without first copying unavailable managed bytes. Normal healthy conversion, recovery conversion and compound conversion share the same one-asset, complete-set and exact-generation rules. ADR 0352 separately governs keeping, releasing or cleaning Retained Ownership Conversion Sources and never hides external-source deletion inside the ownership commit. ADR 0353 separately governs the indefinite retention, explicit complete-set cleanup and non-activating restoration of Managed Ownership Conversion Residue. ADR 0354 permits an exact-generation residue to recover unavailable referenced ownership as managed, while treating prior different content as explicit replacement rather than mislabeling it as ownership conversion. ADR 0355 coordinates normal conversions in one-direction batches without weakening any item, complete-set, cleanup or recovery boundary. ADR 0356 resolves batch-managed referenced destinations and collisions without copying internal storage hierarchy or allowing batch replacement authority. ADR 0357 keeps conversion byte-exact by preserving library metadata separately instead of embedding it or creating sidecars. The current application has no general ownership-conversion plan, retained-source exclusion, managed conversion residue or ownership-conversion recovery. This ADR records target architecture only and reads, stages, copies, validates, relates, converts, restores, moves or deletes no runtime/user file, directory, database, backup, cache or system-trash item and changes no public IPC, database schema or AI Worker API.
