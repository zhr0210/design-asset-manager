# Candidate Identity Precedes Candidate Activation

The Capture Gateway must create and persist Candidate Identity and its Candidate Record as soon as it accepts a Capture Envelope, while keeping that record in Candidate Intake during artifact acquisition and validation. Candidate Intake remains traceable through Capture Feedback and recovery surfaces but does not appear in the Capture Inbox main grid. Candidate Activation occurs only after the complete original passes basic file and integrity validation and is atomically committed as a Candidate Artifact into the active Library Root's Original Asset Storage under ADR 0280; activation makes it an Active Candidate, exposes it in Capture Inbox, and starts Candidate Retention Policy timing. Acquisition Failure preserves the stable identity and failure reason for retry or recovery without creating a broken review card, accepting operational staging as an original, or starting retention.
