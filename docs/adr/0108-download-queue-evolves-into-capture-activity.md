# Download Queue Evolves Into Capture Activity

The transfer-centric Download Queue should evolve into a lightweight contextual Capture Activity Panel for pre-activation capture operations rather than become a fourth permanent Workspace Entry. It shows Candidate Intake, artifact acquisition and validation progress, Acquisition Failure, and retry or cancellation actions, while the underlying Download Task remains transfer detail rather than candidate lifecycle state. Candidate Activation removes successful work from the active panel and hands the candidate to Capture Inbox with Capture Feedback. Capture Attention Badge counts only unresolved acquisition failures, not successful or merely active captures. Capture Activity Panel remains distinct from Capture Inbox Activity Panel, which owns post-activation candidate batch operations and result history, and it excludes AI model, runtime package, and dependency downloads.
